What we build

Most projects start with something specific: a task your team does by hand every week, or a tool you pay for that only does half the job.

Systems

Internal tools and portals built around how your team actually works, instead of a generic SaaS product they have to work around.

  • Replace the six spreadsheets your ops team reconciles every Friday with one app and one source of truth.
  • A job-tracking tool for a field services company that connects dispatch, technician notes and invoicing.
  • A client intake and status portal for a law or accounting firm, so clients stop emailing to ask where their file stands.

Data & Analytics

Pipelines that pull your data into one place automatically, and reports that are already done when Monday starts.

  • Combine order, inventory and ad-spend data from an online store into a warehouse you own.
  • Weekly job-costing reports for a construction company, pulled from accounting and project management without anyone copying and pasting.
  • Dashboards for a group of clinics showing appointment volume, no-shows and payer mix by location.

AI Workflows

Document extraction, summarization and agents for repetitive reading and routing work. Each one ships with tests and logging, so you can see what it did and catch it when it's wrong.

  • Pull line items, dates and totals from freight bills and carrier invoices into your logistics system.
  • Summarize intake calls or consultation notes into a standard format your team can review in seconds.
  • Triage an agency's shared inbox: classify requests, draft replies, and route each one to the right person for approval.

Who it's for

Companies of roughly 10 to 500 people that have outgrown their spreadsheets but don't have a software team.

  • Ops-heavy businessesLogistics, construction, field services and e-commerce, where much of the work is moving information between systems.
  • Professional services firmsLaw, accounting, agencies and clinics, where staff time goes to intake, status updates and paperwork.
  • Founders with a half-built appIt works in a demo, but needs authentication, testing, monitoring and a real deployment before customers depend on it.

How we work

You see the plan and the price before you commit to a build. Each step stands on its own, so you can stop after any of them.

  1. Step 1

    Systems Audit

    A short review of how work and data move through your business today. We talk with the people doing the work and look at the tools and files they use.

    What you get
    Written findings and a ranked list of the opportunities most worth fixing.
  2. Step 2

    Blueprint Sprint

    One to two weeks defining exactly what gets built, before any production code.

    What you get
    A written spec, an architecture plan, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote. The sprint fee is credited toward the build.
  3. Step 3

    Fixed-Price Build

    We build what the blueprint describes, for the price we quoted.

    What you get
    A demo every week, and a finished system deployed in your own cloud account. You own the code, the data and the infrastructure.
  4. Step 4

    Care Plan

    Software needs upkeep after it ships.

    What you get
    Hosting, monitoring and fixes. An Improve retainer adds a standing block of time for new features as the business changes.
